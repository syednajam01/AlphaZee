## 5. Catalog data model

Design a small schema supporting:

**Collection**
- Stable ID.
- Unique slug.
- Name.
- Optional image.
- Active status and display order.

**Product**
- Stable ID and unique slug.
- Title and description.
- Collection association.
- Image URL.
- Active status.
- Optional hero placement and presentation metadata.
- Creation and update timestamps.

**Variant**
- Stable ID and unique SKU.
- Product association.
- Optional size and color.
- Price in integer PKR minor units.
- Active status.
- Explicit availability status.

Every purchasable product must have a variant, including products without visible options.

Represent actual valid size/color combinations as variants. Do not assume all combinations are available. Prevent duplicate combinations for a product.

For now, use manually maintained availability such as available, unavailable, or unknown. Do not invent supplier stock quantities or imply that supplier stock is synchronized.

Use database constraints where appropriate, including nonnegative prices, foreign keys, and uniqueness.

The catalog is the single source of product prices and availability. Hero entries must reference catalog products rather than duplicating commercial data.

Keep image/video files outside PostgreSQL; store URLs only. Object storage setup is out of scope.
